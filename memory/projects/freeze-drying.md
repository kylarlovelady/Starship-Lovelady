# Freeze drying business
Kylar already owns a freeze dryer and has made decent money with it. It takes time (buy, load, wait hours, package, ship); a backup income idea.
- Easiest first product: candy (crowded market). Problem: clear Mylar bags crushed the candy; wants cheap, moisture-proof, tamper-evident rigid containers (small PET jars with induction-sealed lids, or Mylar pouches inside a rigid sleeve); needs more bags.
- Pipeline to build: a simple store takes orders; a label template auto-fills the business name, candy name and required label info for batch printing; postage and addresses come from the order.
- Other ideas: dog treats, cosmetics. Higher-liability services (freeze drying placenta, breast milk) are a separate category; candy first.
