## Reverse-engineering Kalshi's pricing: research 2026-11-06 (Kylar asked)
- Rule (Kalshi contract terms, CF Benchmarks): the value is the simple average of the index over the 60 seconds before expiry, compared with the strike. Missing data at expiry resolves to No. Revisions after expiry do not count. (kalshi-public-docs.s3.amazonaws.com/contract_terms/CRYPTO.pdf)
- OddsShopper (not independently verified): in the last minute the final 15 s are only about a quarter of the average, so a late move must be about 4x the gap to flip it; bots reprice off the same index within seconds (864 to 1,281 fills per 10 s in the final minute of one window); windows that finish within $50 of the line flipped late about 21% of the time, farther than $50 about 1%; the team found no repeatable mispricing in two weeks of testing. This matches our own findings (price already knows; sellers leave).
- GitHub bot ideas worth testing (author claims, unverified): github.com/hamad-khawaja/kalshi-trading-bot (model vs market gap net of fees, edge must persist several cycles, phases of the window, BTC leads ETH, Monte Carlo, fractional Kelly, claims contracts under 25c lose and quiet hours 6 pm to 5 am Eastern lose).
