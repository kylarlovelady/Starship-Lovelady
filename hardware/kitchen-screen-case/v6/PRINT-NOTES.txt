Kitchen screen case v6 FINAL, 38 mm slim build (bare Pi 5 + Active Cooler screwed to the plate): print notes, black ASA
Files (stl_slim/ in the repo; the zip has them): body_left, body_right (plate down), faceplate_left, faceplate_right (face down), door_A, door_B, door_C (stand upright, brim on), feet_x4 (or use rubber pads).
Settings: 0.2 mm layers, 3 perimeters, 15% infill, enclosure closed. Brim on the faceplates and doors. Tabs under the screen carry their own 45 degree gussets, so no support is needed.
Print door_A and body_left first. Check that the door swings and the M3 x 30 pin goes in from the front (top, faceplate off) and threads into the lowest knuckle (2.7 mm hole, the thread cuts itself).
Hardware: 20 x M2.5 x 10 (faceplate, 6 of them also latch the doors), 4 x M2.5 x 4 (screen tabs; 6 mm would poke past a 3 mm standoff), 4 x M2.5 x 16 (join the halves), 3 x M3 x 30 (hinge pins), double-sided tape for the Pi kit case, thin foam tape under the faceplate lip.
Pi: bare Pi 5 with the Active Cooler on the 4 plate posts, 4 x M2.5 x 6 (Kernmax 6 mm) through the Pi holes into the posts; USB-C and micro-HDMI face the left bay. No tape needed.
Cooling path: cool air in through door B (bottom, under the Pi) and door C slots, air passes the fan grille slots in the plate, hot air out the 6 slanted louvers in the top wall. Keep the wall mount from blocking the plate slots (the 4 mm feet leave a gap). Check the Pi temperature after a day: vcgencmd measure_temp, throttling starts near 80 C.
Not confirmed yet: screw hole positions and standoff height on the back of the screen, Pi kit case size and position, plug lengths (HDMI adapter + straight plug needs about 50 mm; the bay gives 26 to 37 mm, so a short flexible right-angle HDMI extension may be needed). Dry fit with the real parts before printing everything.
