Hardware

  • 20 × M2.5 × 10 black cap screws, faceplate (6 of them also latch the doors)
  • 4 × M2.5 × 4 mm (the shortest in the kit is fine), screen to the corner tabs, head down in the back counterbore
  • 4 × M2.5 × 6, Pi 5 to its four plate posts (38 mm build)
  • 4 × M2.5 × 16, joins the two halves
  • 3 × M3 × 30, door hinge pins (yours)
  • One power cord: down the left bay, across the bottom behind the Pi, out the 8 mm slot under the right bay
  • 2 wall screws for the keyholes, 150 mm apart

How the screen stays put

  • 4 corner tabs sit behind the screen at its mounting holes. An M2.5 screw through each tab threads into the screen's own posts.
  • The faceplate lip overlaps the screen's front border by 4 mm, so the screen cannot come forward even if a screw loosens.
  • The pocket walls hold it sideways with 0.4 mm play. A strip of foam tape under the lip stops any rattle.

Hinges

  • 3 knuckles, 9.8 mm each, wall 2.8 mm around the pin. The M3 × 30 passes the top two and threads into the bottom one.
  • The head sits 1.6 mm into the top knuckle and 1.8 mm into the faceplate, so nothing sticks out.
  • To open a door: take out its 2 latch screws in the faceplate, then pry it out with a screwdriver tip or fingernail in the round groove at its free end.
